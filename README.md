## 🚀 Why This Matters

### The Problem
Traditional money transfers are:
- **Complicated** - Forms with 15+ fields, banking jargon, confusing requirements
- **Expensive** - Hidden fees, poor exchange rates, markup charges
- **Slow** - 3-5 business days, manual processing, unclear delivery times
- **Limited** - Support for only 10-20 countries, require multiple apps

### The Solution
Conversational transfers are:
- **Natural** - Just chat. No forms, no learning curve, zero friction
- **Transparent** - Real-time rates from Wise API, upfront fees, no hidden charges
- **Fast** - Seconds to minutes for most transfers, instant confirmations
- **Global** - **166 countries** supported through Wise's world-class infrastructure

## ⚡ How It Works

### 1. **Just Chat**
Talk naturally to Claude, ChatGPT, or WhatsApp. No special commands, no syntax to learn.

### 2. **AI Handles Everything**
The AI understands country requirements (Mexican CLABE, Colombian Cédula, European IBAN, etc.) and asks for what's needed conversationally.

### 3. **Real Transfer, Real Time**
Your transfer executes through Wise API with real exchange rates. Track it with a unique transfer ID.

### 4. **Money Arrives**
Most transfers complete in minutes to hours, not days. Your recipient gets exactly what you see on screen.
